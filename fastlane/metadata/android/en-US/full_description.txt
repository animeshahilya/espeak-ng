<p>eSpeak NG as an Android text-to-speech engine, built for screen reader users. It speaks the way eSpeak speaks in NVDA on Windows. Indian languages get extra pronunciation fixes, and those fixes only affect Indian voices.</p>

<p>Features:</p>

<ul>
<li>152 languages are built in. The app works fully offline and has no internet permission.</li>
<li>Speaks before the phone is unlocked (Direct Boot).</li>
<li>Symbols, punctuation and emoji are read the way NVDA reads them.</li>
<li>Voice variants, pitch, volume up to 200%, and a rate boost for very fast listening.</li>
<li>An audio optimizer for a warmer, fuller sound.</li>
<li>Indian number reading in lakh and crore for Indian voices.</li>
<li>My Words: your own pronunciation dictionary, which can import and export NVDA dictionaries.</li>
<li>Locks that stop other apps from changing your rate, pitch or volume.</li>
<li>Backup and restore for all settings.</li>
<li>Runs on phones and on Wear OS.</li>
</ul>
